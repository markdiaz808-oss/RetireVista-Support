RetireVista Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

RetireVista is a local-first retirement planning and educational scenario-modeling app. Retirement-plan inputs are stored on the user's device. RetireVista does not require an account, and the current production app does not transmit user-entered retirement-plan information to a RetireVista-operated server.

Information You Enter

You may enter household names, ages, retirement dates, financial account balances, income, spending, Social Security estimates, healthcare spending, goals, and other planning assumptions.

This information is used on-device to calculate and display retirement projections, simulations, scenario comparisons, and related educational planning outputs.

Data Collection

The current production version of RetireVista is designed not to collect or transmit user-entered retirement planning information off the device for RetireVista's use.

RetireVista does not sell user-entered retirement planning information or use it for advertising.

Storage and Deletion

Planning information is stored locally using application storage. Users can inspect or export supported locally stored planning records and delete saved planning information from within the app.

Deleting RetireVista from a device may also remove locally stored application data according to iOS behavior.

Third Parties

RetireVista is distributed through Apple's App Store and TestFlight. Apple operates those services under Apple's own privacy practices.

Financial Planning

RetireVista provides educational planning and scenario modeling. Outputs are estimates and simulations, not guarantees, securities recommendations, investment-management services, tax returns, legal advice, insurance advice, or fiduciary advice.
